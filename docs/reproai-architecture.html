Architecture · Runs in prototype · Not clinically validated

One record, one gate, every answer cited

People and clinicians meet one consent barrier. A deterministic model scores assessments and attributes each factor; a retrieval layer answers only from passages it can point to. Nothing reaches a patient until a clinician releases it.

THE HEADLINE

Nothing is generated without a source

Retrieval runs before the model. If the best passage scores too low, no answer is written and the person is told so. Every sentence shown carries the passage it came from.
