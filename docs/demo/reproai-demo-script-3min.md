# ReproAI demo video: 3-minute cut (English)

Same story and recordings as the 4-minute film (`reproai-demo-script.md`), with tighter narration. Beats 7b and 15 reuse lines from the 4-minute film (same voice). Every judging criterion and R1–R6 is still covered: consent (R5), manual/Bluetooth readings with provenance, alerts and triage, population insight (R4), the copilot, the governed next step, the model with its limits (R2), refusing to guess, clinician sign-off, Reem's trajectory and options (R1, R3), FHIR and UAE hosting, measures (R6), and the pilot ask. Native Apple Health / Health Connect access is integrated in code but must be validated on real devices before filming it as a live flow.

All people are fictional; all data is synthetic. Each `> ` line is one narration paragraph.

---

**1 · Meet Reem** · card, then Reem's phone
> This is Reem. She's thirty-one, and not trying to conceive yet. Most people only learn about their reproductive potential once they're trying, or struggling. ReproAI starts that conversation earlier.

**2 · Invitation and intake** · invitation email, then About me
> Her doctor invites her by email; there's no open sign-up. She answers what only she knows: her cycle, her history, her plans, including an interest in egg freezing.

**3 · Her choices** · Privacy and consent
> Then she decides who sees what, and she can change it at any time. Her data is never used for employment or insurance.

**4 · Her devices** · Health tracking, Bluetooth thermometer, sources
> Her temperature and sleep can be entered by hand or read from a Bluetooth thermometer. The native app can also read Apple Health or Health Connect with her permission; if no readings are available, it offers manual entry. Every saved reading keeps its source.

**5 · Handover** · phone to desktop
> All of it travels, with her choices, to her doctor.

**6 · Alerts and triage** · dashboard, then follow-up board
> Her doctor sees the whole panel, and alerts raised from people's own readings: sent to the care team, not as an alarm to the patient, and tracked from alert, to appointment, to closed. If nothing is booked within fourteen days, it's flagged as lost.

**7 · Population insight**
> Zooming out, population insight shows which groups in her care would benefit from earlier advice, with small groups hidden.

**7b · Reem's record** · Reem's case: verified labs, preservation flag citing Thiqa
> In Reem's record, every result shows its source and date. Because she mentioned egg freezing, a preservation discussion is flagged, citing the Thiqa guideline.

**8 · The copilot**
> Before her next visit, the doctor asks the clinical copilot about Reem. It answers from her record and cited guidance, and checks every sentence against its source.

**9 · The next step, governed**
> It can also propose the next step, with its reasons, but nothing happens until the doctor approves.

**10 · The model**
> Then the doctor runs the assessment. The model is deterministic and literature-derived: it shows its inputs and where each came from, an estimate with its range, and the factors behind it, for clinicians only, labelled not yet validated.

**11 · When data is missing** · Layla
> And if a required result is missing, it refuses to guess.

**12 · The release**
> Nothing reaches Reem until the doctor writes a note, and approves it.

**13 · What Reem sees** · phone, trajectory and options, then Arabic
> On her phone, Reem sees how her picture is changing, her doctor's note, and options to discuss, each with its source, in English or Arabic. Not a score, and not a diagnosis.

**13b · Reem asks** · phone assistant, cited answer
> And when she asks a question, the assistant answers only from cited guidance, and tells her when it doesn't know.

**14 · For the health system** · evidence page
> Beyond one clinic, ReproAI exports standard FHIR records, runs its language model in the UAE, and measures the days from first signal to counselling, with honest intervals.

**15 · The ask** · pilot card, then end card
> ReproAI is ready for a pilot: one primary-care cluster and one fertility centre, for six months. We're looking for that partner. Earlier signals, the right next step, with the clinician in control.
