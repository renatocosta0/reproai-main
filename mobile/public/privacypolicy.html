ReproAI health data privacy

ReproAI accesses sleep and basal body-temperature data only after you enable ReproAI wearable-data consent and approve the separate permission shown by Apple Health or Health Connect.

How readings are used

When you choose to sync, the app reads the previous 30 days and sends returned readings to your ReproAI care record to show trends and support follow-up with your care team. ReproAI does not write readings back to Apple Health or Health Connect. These readings are not used for advertising or as an input to the fertility estimate.

Your choice and control

You can deny the operating-system permission or turn off wearable-data consent in ReproAI. Turning off consent prevents later imports; it does not automatically delete readings already stored in your ReproAI record. Contact your care provider for questions about access or deletion of stored records.

Health readings are handled under the privacy terms of the ReproAI service and the provider operating your care record.

This summary is specific to the mobile health-data integration.
